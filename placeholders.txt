What are we celebrating?

Grundlovsdag (Constitution Day)
in Denmark

What are we drinking?

Danish øl (beer), typically a cold Carlsberg or Tuborg lager


How are we drinking it?

Ideally outside, on a picnic blanket with friends, passing around cold beers and drinking from the can. Celebrate your friends and their ambitious political ideas with a toast of "Skål!"

Why are we celebrating?

Grundlovsdag commemorates the signing of Denmark's first constitution on June 5, 1849, which transformed the country into a constitutional monarchy and granted citizens fundamental rights. It remains a national public holiday celebrated with outdoor gatherings, civic speeches, and a relaxed, festive atmosphere across the country.
